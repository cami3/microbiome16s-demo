Downstream analyses were performed with Microbiome16S v1.0.0 (R engine 1.0.0; R version 4.6.1 (2026-06-24)). Features classified as unassigned, mitochondrial or chloroplast were removed (45 features, 1.81% of reads). Samples were rarefied without replacement to 1,328 reads per sample (random seed 42); 27 of 34 samples were retained (7 below the rarefaction depth were excluded). Samples were compared across 4 groups of 'body-site' (gut, left palm, right palm, tongue). Rarefied data were used for composition, alpha and beta diversity; unrarefied counts of the retained samples were used for differential abundance, regression and correlation analyses.

Alpha diversity (observed features, Shannon index, Gini-Simpson index, Pielou's evenness, Faith's phylogenetic diversity) was compared between groups with the Kruskal-Wallis test followed by Dunn's post-hoc test with Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) correction; effect sizes are reported as eta-squared (eta2[H]).

Relative abundances were summarised at phylum and genus level. Taxa present in at least 10% of samples were compared between groups with the Kruskal-Wallis test, with BH correction across taxa.

Beta diversity was quantified with the Jaccard distance (presence/absence), the Bray-Curtis dissimilarity, unweighted UniFrac and weighted UniFrac. UniFrac distances were computed on the rooted phylogenetic tree after resolving polytomies into zero-length dichotomies (ape::multi2di). Community structure was visualised by principal coordinates analysis (PCoA) and non-metric multidimensional scaling (NMDS, Jaccard (presence/absence)). Group differences were tested by PERMANOVA (vegan::adonis2, 999 permutations), and homogeneity of multivariate dispersion with betadisper and a permutation test. Pairwise PERMANOVA was performed for every pair of groups with BH correction.

Differential abundance was assessed at genus level for genera present in at least 10% of samples, using two complementary approaches: the Kruskal-Wallis test on relative abundances and on centred log-ratio (CLR; pseudocount 0.5) transformed counts, each with BH correction across genera. Genera significant in both approaches (q < 0.05) were considered consensus hits; Dunn's test (BH) on CLR values identified the differing pairs of groups.

The association between individual genera and group membership (left palm vs gut) was explored with univariate logistic regression on standardised CLR abundances (odds ratio per 1 SD, 95% Wald confidence intervals, BH-adjusted p-values); models showing complete or quasi-complete separation were flagged as unreliable. Discriminative performance was estimated as the area under the ROC curve with 5-fold stratified cross-validation repeated 20 times. Because candidate genera were selected on the full dataset, these AUC values are exploratory and may be optimistic.

Spearman correlations between CLR-transformed abundances of the 30 most abundant genera and numeric metadata (month, day, days-since-experiment-start) were computed, with BH false discovery rate correction across all genus-variable pairs.

Statistical significance was set at a BH-adjusted p (q) < 0.05.
